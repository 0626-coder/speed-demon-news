**Speed-Demon News** is a simple racing news site. The idea is straightforward: pull trustworthy headlines from public feeds, give each one clear source credit, and leave room to publish original articles without standing up a heavy CMS.

Street, no-prep, and grudge lead the front page. Drag racing — NHRA, Stock and Super Stock Eliminator, and the broader sportsman world — sits right behind that, with dirt of every class, NASCAR, Formula 1, IndyCar, and IMSA in the next lanes. The site can grow without a redesign.

## How this is meant to run

Rebuild the site with one command when you want fresh headlines. Original stories live as Markdown files in `content/posts`. There is a local form and a small script to create those files. Nothing here requires a daily login, a plugin update, or a paid host.

Headlines on this site are short excerpts taken from the source feeds. We do not copy full articles. Every card links out to the original piece.

If you are here for the Grudge Board, start at the top of the homepage or in Street / Grudge. For Stock Eliminator, engine tech, or the sportsman pits, hit Drag Racing and the Engines / Tech filter. If you want the rest of the weekend, the other series are one click away.
